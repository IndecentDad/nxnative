- This is downstream of the IPA request. Confirm the installed app's provisioning profile and executable signature contain the entitlement; setting the capability in the developer account alone may not mean SideStore included it in its installed profile. A reported workaround was enabling the App ID capability through Xcode, reimporting the IPA that carries the request, then reinstalling through SideStore. If the account is paid or the entitlement is present in the installed signature, investigate a different path.
